DOSEBUDDY / DEMO

Try DoseBuddy.

Keep track of medications, see your progress and bring your notes to your next appointment.

A few things to try

  1. Record a dose, then correct it in Insights → tools → Dose Review.
  2. Open the calendar and compare the week and month adherence charts.
  3. Add a symptom, log a refill, or create a medication with the unit selector.
  4. Preview and download a doctor report with symptoms included.

Sample records are ready to explore. Reset clears your changes. Use sample information only.

Demo details

Upload a sample medication label or try the example text. Review the results before saving.

Notifications require the phone app. Family sharing and invitations are not available in this demo.
